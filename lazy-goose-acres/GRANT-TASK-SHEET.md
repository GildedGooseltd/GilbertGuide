## Import CSV to your Sheet (2 min)

1. Open the [live tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/15RoaOaeYmbXsBi0LflbAfOgwd7brcPypullu8SXXl2M/edit?gid=2085542785)
2. **File → Import → Upload** → select `daily/sheets/grant-goose-tasks.csv`
3. Import location: **Append to current sheet** (first time) or **Replace current sheet** (full refresh)
4. Separator: **Comma** · Convert text to numbers: **No**

After import, sort or filter by **Status** (Open / Wait / Hold / Done) and **Priority** (P1 first).
