## 2. Healthy Food Access Plan 

### Problem

Huerfano County is a **low-income, low-access (LILA)** area. Many residents in Walsenburg, Gardner, and surrounding rural tracts travel **20+ miles** to reach a full-service grocery with reliable produce, dairy, and fresh protein. Convenience stores dominate nearby; **healthy staple foods** are limited to 2 stores with short business hours. 

### Applicant

**Gilded Goose LLC** is a woman-owned Colorado business operated by Kate, with a background in technology operations and small-business systems. The business is pivoting from short-term confectionery vending toward **wholesale distribution and unattended healthy-food retail** — a model suited to low-population rural areas where staffing a full store is not viable.

**Lazy Goose Acres** (36 acres, Gardner) Currently building up a small local farm. Start addressing food resourcing with grocery delivery and pickup options available to function as a farmstand. Gilded Goose buys at wholesale and stages cold inventory for retail nodes.

### Solution

A **three-part system**:

1. **Wholesale cold hub (FOOD-14)** — Commercial freezers at an agreed street address to receive bulk frozen and refrigerated goods.
2. **Unattended retail nodes (FOOD-13, FOOD-22)** — Smart refrigerated units stocked with ≥70% eligible staples (produce, dairy, eggs, bread, frozen vegetables, grains). Card and SNAP checkout; no cash; remote monitoring.
3. **Restock logistics (FOOD-08, FOOD-24)** — Scheduled weekly routes from hub to nodes; refrigerated commercial vehicle or trailer for cold chain integrity — **parked selling**, not a staffed rolling store.

**Phase 0 (underway):** Community pre-order board (FOOD-07) tests demand before equipment spend.

### Who benefits

- Walsenburg residents without reliable grocery access, including SNAP households (FOOD-01, FOOD-02).
- Gardner and Highway 12 corridor residents — **24/7 access** at a parked node 
- Local growers (FOOD-05) who can consign through hub/trading post without operating their own retail.

### Healthy food definition (our commitment)

**In scope:** Fresh and frozen produce, dairy, eggs, meat, bread, rice, beans, oats, frozen vegetables, water.  
**Out of scope:** Candy, soda, energy drinks, tobacco, non-food items in vending nodes.

Existing coin candy machines at the mercantile are **being wound down** and are **not** part of this project or credit request.

### Equipment → impact map


| Equipment                   | How it increases healthy food access                                    |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Commercial freezers         | Lower unit cost via wholesale buy; keep frozen staples in stock locally |
| Smart fridge / vending node | 24/7 access to refrigerated staples in LILA tracts                      |
| Generator                   | Prevents spoilage during outages — food stays available after storms    |
| SNAP/POS kiosk              | SNAP/WIC households can purchase eligible staples                       |
| Reefer truck/trailer        | Reliable cold delivery to underserved nodes; extends geographic reach   |
