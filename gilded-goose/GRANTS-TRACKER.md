**Goal (phased):**

2. **Phase B — Grant-funded build (later):** OP-06 agrivoltaics grant or NEXT-YR-02 ACRE3 for demo/education on **unused acres** — only after Phase A proves the model.
3. **Phase C — Resale / related products (later):** Mercantile or mobile sales of **related items** (efficiency kits, small solar accessories, educational materials, farm store add-ons) — not full dealer franchise upfront.

---

### What partners are *for*

| Partner type | What they want from you | What you get |
|--------------|-------------------------|--------------|
| **Solar developer** (lease your land) | 15–40 yr lease on **unused** flat/clear acres; access for construction | **Annual lease $** (~$300–$1,200/ac CO range); keep ownership; often agrivoltaics grazing/habitat |
| **CALC / SALSA** (existing solar sites) | Farmer to graze, apiary, or small ag **under panels** on someone else’s array | ~$1/yr land inside array; coaching; possible startup $ case-by-case |
| **SIEA (your coop)** | Members adding approved grid-tied solar; community programs | Net metering path; ask about community solar subscriptions + **education workshops** |
| **AgriSolar / CALC (grant subcontractors)** | Outreach partner for workshops in SE CO (Huerfano = **+5 pts** on OP-06) | Paid subcontract on grant; no array capital |
| **Equipment vendor (Phase C)** | Dealer margin on kits they ship | You sell related goods at mercantile; start small |

**Wind:** Few turnkey “host a turbine on 36 ac” programs. Practical path = **solar first** (coop + lease + grants), small wind only if OP-06/ACRE3 funds a **demo + education** story.

---

### How land leasing for solar works (simple)

1. **Developer contacts you** OR you submit land info (COSSA developer directory, Pivot/Cloudbreak landowner forms).
2. **Feasibility:** Grid proximity, SIEA interconnection, slope, water rights, Huerfano **panel height** (county may approve taller arrays case-by-case).
3. **Option / lease:** Often 1–2 yr option to study, then **15–40 yr lease**. You stay **landowner**; developer builds, owns, operates array.
4. **Payment:** Per-acre annual rent, often with escalation. Paid whether or not you graze under panels.
5. **Agrivoltaics add-on:** Many CO developers now plan **grazing/pollinator** under panels — your farm story helps their permit.
6. **Before signing:** Get lease reviewed; confirm ag use, hunting access, roads, decommissioning bond, property tax.

**You do not need money to start a lease conversation** — only acres you’re willing to commit (unused portions of 36 ac).
