## Eligibility gates (must pass before writing)

1. **LILA** — Business located in or serves low-income, low-access area (Huerfano qualifies; confirm mercantile address + delivery routes on HFFI/USDA LILA maps).
2. **Small food retailer** — &lt;10,000 sf; carries ≥3 federal staple categories (produce, meat, dairy, bread, etc.).
3. **Small family farm** (if farm track) — CO-owned/operated; gross &lt;$350K; sales to LILA retailers (not restaurants-only, not candy-only).
4. **Healthy food** — Equipment must increase access to or lower prices for **healthy food**. **Excluded:** candy, soda, office supplies, installation, salaries, food inventory.
5. **Purchase timing** — Equipment must be **purchased and paid in 2026** before application. Letter of Eligibility can be requested **before** purchase.
6. **First-time credit** — Priority if never received CFA tax credit before.
