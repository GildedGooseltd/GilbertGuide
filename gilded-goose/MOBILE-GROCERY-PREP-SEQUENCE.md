## FAQ — E911, cold building, using someone else’s address

### What is E911?

**E911 (Enhanced 911)** is the **official street number and road name** emergency services use to find a property. Mail, Google Maps, SNAP, and the health department usually want this — not “36 ac near Gardner” or a legal description.

In Huerfano County, **vacant land without a completed home typically does not get a new E911 address**. That is why you need another **approved street address** for licenses and retail programs.

### What is a “cold building”?

A **cold building** here means a **non-house structure used only for temperature-controlled food storage** — for example:

- Used **reefer trailer** or box truck (plug-in cold storage)
- Prefab **walk-in cooler** or insulated cold room
- Commercial **reach-in freezers** under a roof (shed/barn)

It is **not** a dwelling. You are not living there. OP-01 equipment credit can apply to **purchased** cold equipment (trailer, freezers, generator) — not digging a root cellar or paying install labor alone.

Cold storage must sit at an address the **health department approves** (often the community center with permission, not random vacant land without an address).

### Using someone else’s property as your **business / operating address**

| Use | Someone else’s property OK? | Rules |
|-----|----------------------------|--------|
| **Customer pickup / “store location”** | **Yes, with written permission** | Raymond Aguirre CC (28 CR 632) + county letter is the clean path |
| **SNAP “store location”** | **Yes** — physical address where food is **sold to the public** | Mailing address can differ (PO box OK for mail only) |
| **Retail food license** | **Yes** — inspected site must match where food is stored/sold | LA-H Health approves the specific address |
| **LOE / LILA map pin** | **Yes** — pin where project **serves** customers | Must match your real operating story |
| **CO SOS / IRS mailing** | **Yes** — registered agent or PO box | Does not replace operating address |
| **Wholesale **delivery drop**** | **Yes** — supplier ships where you authorize | Delivery address ≠ business address on all forms |
| **Friend’s home as “the store” on forms** | **Risky** | Residential zoning, insurance, inspections, owner liability — only if they **agree in writing** and health dept licenses **that** address |
| **Friend’s home for shipping boxes only** | **Sometimes** — logistics only | OK for **unopened wholesale freight** if they accept deliveries; **not** a substitute for licensed pickup site unless health dept says so |
| **Offsite storage in someone’s garage** | **Only if licensed** | Must be on your food plan; inspector can reject residential storage |
| **Virtual mailbox (UPS Store etc.)** | **No** for SNAP store location or food license site | Fine for some mail |

**Rule of thumb:**  
- **Operating address** = where customers get food (community center).  
- **Mailing address** = where **you** get mail (PO box, your residence in another town, registered agent).  
- **Delivery address** = where the **truck drops pallets** (can be community center if permitted — ask Lester Berry + health dept).

You **cannot** borrow an address on paper without the **owner’s permission** and **regulator approval** for food use.

### Can I use someone else’s **home** for shipping and offsite storage?

| Scenario | Practical answer |
|----------|------------------|
| **Wholesaler ships to their porch/garage** | OK as a **one-off** if they accept freight — put their name on the delivery appointment. Does **not** make that your legal food establishment. |
| **Ongoing commercial cold storage in their garage** | Treat as a **licensed food storage site** — needs owner OK, insurance, health dept approval. Many residential garages **fail** inspection for retail food storage. |
| **Your mail/packages to their address** | Fine for personal mail; **do not** list as SNAP store location unless you are licensed to sell food there. |
| **Better option** | **28 CR 632** community center for pickup + staged storage with county letter; reefer trailer on site. |
