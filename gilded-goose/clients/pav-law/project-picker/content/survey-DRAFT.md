## Optional longer series (5 prompts, menu-style)

Use if the card should list questions (like the paper mockup) instead of one branching prompt:

1. **Where do you want more signed cases from?** — paid search, display/brand, seasonal, organic/SEO  
2. **What happens after someone reaches out?** — phones/VoIP, after-hours chat, CRM pipeline, outbound dials  
3. **Is the website earning its keep?** — SEO/content, speed/nav, landing pages  
4. **How do past clients come back or refer?** — referral program, testimonials, mailers, directory/social profiles  
5. **What must get clearer or cheaper?** — KPI dashboard, priorities audit, subscription leak, ad waste
