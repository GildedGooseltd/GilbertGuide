### What to improve first · useful + more traffic

Ranked for this project. Case-value context from MyCase Client fee means: sex assault ~$9,500 · DV ~$5,400 · DUI ~$3,500. Photo-radar tickets are civil — use that post to route criminal traffic / DUI, not to chase $40 citations.

1. Photo-radar post — already #5 landing and the only blog post that behaves like a magnet. Expand: El Paso / COS if cameras expand · red-light / school-zone / work-zone AVIS · what is civil vs reckless / DUI. CTA should split “pay a camera ticket” from “criminal traffic / DUI — call.” Internal links to `/areas-of-practice/dui-dwai` and traffic pages.

2. DUI/DWAI pillar + Express Consent cluster — 712 landings on the pillar. Competitors that win Google for Colorado DUI own standalone “first 24 hours,” “how to request a DMV hearing,” and first vs repeat vs felony DUI pages. Pav already has a strong Express Consent post with 82-second reads and only 9 landings — retitle and meta for “request DMV Express Consent hearing Colorado” · add mydmv.colorado.gov steps · 7-day breath/refusal vs mailed blood 10-day · officer-present tactic. Promote it from the DUI pillar FAQ. Add a first-24-hours / 7-day checklist post. Split first-offense vs repeat / PDD / ignition interlock into supporting posts, not one more generic DUI overview.

3. Protection orders pillar + DV cluster — 716 + 258 landings. This is Pav’s organic practice-page win. Competitors such as Bussey and McDowell treat DV as a tag plus a mandatory protection order, not a vague “domestic violence lawyer” page. Ship posts: what happens at El Paso first appearance · temporary vs permanent CPO · firearms / Lautenberg · accidental violation. Existing thin posts `what-is-mandatory-arrest-in-colorado` and `dont-let-a-dv-conviction-ruin-your-career` have 4–7 landings — rewrite to El Paso procedure, don’t start new URLs.

4. Active-duty military page — 14 landings and 4.6-second engagement. Live page has an empty “High-Risk Charges” list. Schwaner and Right Law rank Fort Carson / Peterson / USAFA DUI + Veterans Trauma Court. Pav already spends Search on Military. Rewrite the practice page, then blog: Fort Carson off-post DUI · VTC eligibility in the 4th JD · Lautenberg + base weapons · clearance self-report. Highest strategic gap vs ads spend.

5. Sexual assault practice + Andrew story — 73 landings / 42s on the practice URL; Andrew story has more internal views than landings. Keep discreet. One useful FAQ cluster, not a blog spray. Pairs with AdEnhance landing work.

6. Green-card DUI post — 64 sessions / 35s. Keep and link hard from the DUI immigration FAQ. Don’t build a full immigration blog; one maintained post is enough.

### Do not prioritize

- `/blog` hub redesign before clusters exist — 6 landings, 0s. Date dump with “say that five times, fast!” is not a consult path.
- County service-area clones — thin, duplicate, almost no engagement.
- History-of-DUI and similar brand posts — 3 landings.
- U-Haul novelty — fun read, not a practice line.
- New photo-radar clones in every suburb until the COS/El Paso angle is on the existing URL.

### Usefulness standard for every new or rewritten URL

- Answers one panicked search in the first screen: deadline, court, or “what happens next.”
- Colorado + El Paso / 4th JD specificity, not national filler.
- Click-to-call + one primary CTA.
- Internal link to the matching practice pillar.
- Do not publish until the military high-risk list and Express Consent meta are fixed — those are live pages already wasting demand.
