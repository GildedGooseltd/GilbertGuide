### Transcribe a video in Google Drive

pav.law is Google Workspace Business Standard. Video captions are included. The AI purchase page that opens from the Drive storage screen is not required. That page is the SKU GOOGLE.GEN_AI_GEMNOVA. It is not Google Voice, and it does not add transcription.

1. Upload the video to Drive.
2. Open the video. Choose More, then Manage caption tracks.
3. If a caption track is already there, download it. That file is the timed transcript.
4. If no track is there, choose Generate automatic captions, wait, then download the track.
5. Once captions exist, Ask Gemini on the open video for a summary. Business Standard includes that.

Captions stay with that file version. Replacing the video drops them. Add captions again on the new version.

Admin control: Admin console, Apps, Google Workspace, Drive and Docs, Features and Applications, Automatically-generated captions. Three settings: generate on upload, require a request per video, or off. Default is generate on upload.

### Outlook files

Drive does not open Outlook mail as mail. A .eml or .msg is one message. A .pst or .ost is a whole mailbox. The Gemini storage purchase does not fix this.

Options, not a pick yet:

- A few messages: open the .msg or .eml in Outlook on a computer that still has it.
- Keepers only: from Outlook, save each message as PDF or .eml, then put those files in Drive. PDF opens anywhere. .eml opens in a mail app.
- A mailbox file: open the .pst in Outlook, or with a one-time reader on one computer, and export only the messages worth keeping. Do not park the .pst in Drive and expect it to search like Gmail.
- A whole mailbox into Gmail is a migration. That stays outside this fee, same as a file-system move.

### Audio transcriptions

Drive captions are for video files, not a voice memo.

Options:

- Google Meet: if Transcribe was on, the transcript is already a Doc in the organizer's Drive.
- Use the plan already paid for: put the audio into a short video, upload that video, and run Drive captions. The download is a timed caption file, not a clean document.
- Microsoft 365, if a seat exists: Word, Home, Dictate, Transcribe, then upload the audio. Works with wav, mp4, m4a, and mp3. A normal Microsoft 365 seat is capped at 300 minutes of uploaded audio a month. Word for Windows in a commercial tenant, or Word on the web in Edge or Chrome. Microsoft receives the audio to do the job and does not keep it after.
- Do not buy GOOGLE.GEN_AI_GEMNOVA for audio. Gemini in the Drive viewer is for PDF and video.
