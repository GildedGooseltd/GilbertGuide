## Description

Pav Law is running Google Ads, LSA, HubSpot, referral, outbound, website, and SEO work simultaneously — without a ranked plan, consulting spend scatters. A focused marketing audit session ranks every channel by ROI, effort, and what depends on what else.

A marketing audit ranks channels by what is working, what is leaking, and what should wait. HubSpot’s audit checklist is a practical framework for professional services firms juggling paid, organic, CRM, and referral at once. See HubSpot — marketing audit checklist and Prioritization frameworks.
