## Comparison verdict

Retainer $2,700/mo
Sits mid–upper for Colorado Springs generalist PPC-only shops (~$1k–$2k) and mid for Denver law-firm PPC management ($1.5k–$5k).
Justified vs local $2,100 “full stack” bundles because Pav scope includes LSA lead ops, Microsoft, criminal-defense creative/policy risk, event support, and intake/CRM coordination — not Maps-only local service PPC.

Project fees $1,000–$3,800
Align with Front Range project/sprint pricing (CRM builds, email campaigns, profile audits). Not cheap consumer SMB package pricing; appropriate for law-firm ops.

HubSpot / CRM packaging
A3 Summer Safety Email ($1,200) is a child of HubSpot Marketing Setup (B13), not a standalone agency category. Comparable Cos Springs shops often fold holiday email into a HubSpot or “email nurture” monthly add-on ($500–$1,500/mo) rather than à la carte.

Watch-outs
If comparing apple-to-apple to a $2,100 Cos Springs bundle, confirm whether that quote includes LSA dispute/ops, call-process training, MyCase→HubSpot hygiene, and live event activation — most local packages do not.
If ad spend stays ~$20k/mo, industry % models (15–20%) would imply $3,000–$4,000 management — GGL retainer alone is under that band; project work above retainer is additive.

## How to use in Gilbert / client talks

Quote retainer as Mid-market Colorado Springs ops for multi-platform law ads + LSA hygiene — not a $999 SMB ads package.
Package HubSpot children (A3, B7, B1, A5) under B13 when presenting fees so holiday email is not mistaken as an orphan upsell.
Keep media spend separate on every invoice and Guide Results line.
