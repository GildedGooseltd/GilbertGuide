## Part 1 — How to update lead information in Google LSA

**Where:** [Google Local Services](https://ads.google.com/localservices) → **Leads** (or the Local Services app).

### Daily checklist (every business day)

1. Open **Leads** and sort by **Newest**.
2. For each new call or message:
   - Open the lead.
   - Confirm **name, phone, service type, and location** match what you heard or read.
   - Add **notes** (short): e.g. “DUI, court date 8/15, wants consult,” “Wrong number,” “Out of area — El Paso.”
3. Set **lead status** (wording in UI may vary slightly):
   - **Booked** — consult scheduled (note date/time in notes).
   - **Archived / Not booked** — spoke, no appointment yet (note reason: price, timing, not a fit).
   - **Spam / Not a lead** — wrong number, solicitor, duplicate, out of service area.
   - **Needs follow-up** — missed call or message not yet returned (Romina or Casey owns callback).
4. If Google asks **“Was this lead booked?”** or similar survey prompts, answer **accurately** — this affects billing and account quality.
5. For **charged leads** you dispute (spam, wrong service, duplicate): use Google’s **dispute** flow in the lead detail; note dispute in HubSpot if the lead was logged there.

### What good notes look like

- One line on **practice area** (DUI, traffic, military, etc.).
- **Outcome:** booked / callback needed / not a fit / spam.
- **Who spoke** (Casey, Romina, Andrew) and **date**.

### Reference

- [Manage Local Services leads](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6224841)
- [Lead status and billing](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6224841) (Google Help — search “mark lead booked” if menu labels change)

## Part 3 — Phone coverage schedule (Casey ↔ Romina)

**Purpose:** One person owns **all inbound calls** during a block so the other can do non-phone work (HubSpot, follow-ups, mail, admin) without splitting attention.

### Recommended ramp (pick one path)

| Phase | Casey | Romina | Duration |
| ----- | ----- | ------ | -------- |
| **A — Start** | **2–3 hours/day** (same window daily, e.g. 9–12) — all calls in window | Other hours + non-phone tasks | 2–3 weeks |
| **B — Scale** | **One full day/week** (e.g. Wednesday) — all calls that day | Full focus off phones that day | When Casey passes shadow review |
| **C — Steady** | Add second half-day or second weekday as comfort grows | Fixed “deep work” days on calendar | Ongoing |

**Rules:**

- During Casey’s block, **Romina does not answer** unless Casey escalates (use team agreement: Slack/text “need you on this line”).
- Outside Casey’s block, **Romina** (or Andrew) owns phones unless schedule says otherwise.
- **No gap:** coverage hours must match what’s set on GBP/LSA and HubSpot routing (see B2 when VoIP transfer completes).

### Success before expanding hours

- LSA statuses updated same day.
- HubSpot tasks created for every booked consult.
- Andrew spot-checks 5 calls/week; feedback within 48 hours.
