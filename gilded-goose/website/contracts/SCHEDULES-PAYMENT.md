## SAVINGS-SHARE RIDER — FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT

Attach this rider to a P0, P1, or P2 project SOW when Consultant performs a financial audit, cost-reduction implementation, vendor renegotiation, or cash recovery.

1. **Savings-share rate.** In addition to the SOW’s fixed List Fee, Client will pay Consultant **20% of Verified Net Savings or Recovered Cash** attributable to Consultant’s documented recommendation or implementation.
2. **Signed baseline.** Before each change, the parties will record the vendor/service, pre-change charge or trailing 90-day average, change date, expected post-change cost, direct implementation costs, and measurement period in a savings register approved by email or signature.
3. **Verified Net Savings.** Pre-change baseline cost minus actual post-change cost, less direct implementation costs, early-termination charges, replacement costs, taxes, and third-party fees.
4. **Recovered Cash.** A refund, billing credit, disputed charge, duplicate payment, or other amount actually received or posted to Client’s account.
5. **Measurement periods.**
   - Subscription, SaaS seat, phone line, domain, hosting, directory, or other recurring-service cancellation / downgrade / consolidation: **12 months from the effective change date**.
   - Vendor, telecom, insurance, processing, or other continuing contract-rate reduction: **6 months from the effective change date**.
   - Advertising, LSA, staffing-schedule, or operating-process revision with usage-variable spend: **3 months after implementation**, adjusted for comparable volume.
   - One-time refund or credit: once, when received or posted.
6. **Invoices.** Recurring savings shares are invoiced **quarterly in arrears** using statements, vendor invoices, or account credits. One-time recoveries are invoiced after posting. Payment is Net **[15]**.
7. **No savings; reversal.** No implemented change or no realized savings means no savings-share fee. If Client reverses a recurring change, accrual stops on the reversal date.
8. **No double-counting.** Overlapping changes and recoveries are counted once. Amounts already included in another fee or credited against the fixed List Fee are excluded.
9. **Exclusions.** Savings caused only by reduced business volume, client-only decisions, market-price changes, taxes, debt principal, legal fees, settlements, case value, trust funds, or Firm revenue are excluded. This rider never compensates Consultant for a legal referral or client outcome.
10. **Survival.** Client’s payment and records obligations survive project completion or termination only through each applicable 12-, 6-, or 3-month measurement period.
11. **Records.** Client will provide the statements and invoices reasonably needed to verify each calculation. If records are delayed, invoicing waits until verification rather than using an estimate.

## DEFAULT / COLLECTIONS FOOTER (OPTIONAL ON ANY PLAN)

If any amount under this Schedule remains unpaid after its due date, Consultant may send a demand letter, suspend services, accelerate unpaid amounts as allowed by the MSA, refer the balance to a collection agency, and file in small claims, county court, district court, or another court of competent jurisdiction. Client will pay reasonable collection costs and attorney fees to the extent permitted by the MSA and applicable law.
