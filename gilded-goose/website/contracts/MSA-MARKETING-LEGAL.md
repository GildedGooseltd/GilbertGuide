ARTICLE 1. AGREEMENT STRUCTURE

1.1 Master Agreement; SOWs. This Agreement governs all services Consultant provides to Client. Each engagement will be described in a written Statement of Work (“SOW”) referencing this Agreement.

1.2 Order of Control. If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement on fees, schedule, or scope, the SOW controls for that engagement. Payment schedules, bonus schedules, and exhibits attached to an SOW are part of that SOW.

1.3 Co-Client Liability. The Firm and the Individual Co-Client are jointly and severally liable for all Client obligations under this Agreement and each SOW, including fees, deferred balances, premiums, pass-through costs, late charges, collection costs, transition fees, and other amounts owed. Consultant may seek payment from either or both Co-Clients without first proceeding against the other.

1.4 Bankruptcy / Insolvency. Co-Client liability is intended to survive Firm dissolution, insolvency, assignment for benefit of creditors, or bankruptcy to the maximum extent permitted by law. The Individual Co-Client signs in an individual capacity and not solely as an officer, owner, employee, or agent of the Firm.

————————————————————————

ARTICLE 2. SERVICES AND COSTS

2.1 Services. Consultant will provide services described in each SOW.

2.2 Media Spend. Advertising/media spend, platform charges, sponsorships, SaaS seats, postage, and similar third-party vendor invoices are Client’s cost and are billed directly to Client unless an SOW expressly says otherwise.

2.3 Physical / Production Hard Costs. Items that must be designed, printed, fabricated, purchased, shipped, picked up, staged, or physically managed may be paid by Client directly or advanced/coordinated by Consultant and invoiced as pass-through costs under Article 5.

2.4 Independent Contractor. Consultant is an independent contractor and is not Client’s employee, partner, co-counsel, or law-firm employee.

————————————————————————

ARTICLE 3. LAW-FIRM ADVERTISING AND ETHICS

3.1 Vendor Compensation. Consultant’s compensation is for marketing and business operations services. Fees must be reasonable for the project type, skill level, scope, timeline, and local market context.

3.2 Attorney Responsibility. Client retains ultimate responsibility for all communications about Client’s legal services, including ads, landing pages, websites, email, SMS, chat, social posts, directories, scripts, and any other public-facing materials.

3.3 Attorney Review Before Publication. Consultant will not publish or place public-facing creative or claims until Client, the approving attorney, or an attorney-designated reviewer approves them in writing (email sufficient). Consultant will deliver materials for review. Client must review within twenty-four (24) hours unless Client states a different review window in writing for that delivery. Failure to respond may pause schedule; it does not transfer compliance responsibility to Consultant.

3.4 No Fee-Splitting; No Referral Fee. Consultant’s fees are fixed fees, retainers, pass-through charges, deferred premiums, fixed change-orders, fixed-dollar operational bonuses, or a savings-based fee expressly stated in an SOW and Savings-Share Rider. A savings-based fee may be calculated only from verified reductions in Client’s operating costs or money actually recovered; it may not be calculated from legal fees, settlements, case value, trust funds, or Firm revenue. Nothing in this Agreement pays Consultant for recommending Client’s legal services or for a specific person becoming a legal client.

3.5 KPI Bonuses; Signed Case Rate. Client may use signed-case rate internally as a reporting metric. However, Consultant’s default bonus schedules will not be based directly on the number of signed cases, signed clients, legal fees collected, or any percentage of legal revenue. Any bonus must be a fixed-dollar amount tied to marketing/operations metrics such as response time, qualified lead documentation, consultation booking rate, answer rate, CRM completeness, reporting deadlines, campaign launch milestones, or cost-per-qualified-lead thresholds. A bonus tied directly to signed-case rate may be used only if Client’s ethics counsel approves the structure in writing before the SOW is signed.

3.6 Required Lawyer Identification. Public advertising prepared under this Agreement will include the name and contact information of at least one responsible lawyer or law firm as Client directs and applicable rules require.

3.7 Truthful, Not Misleading. Client warrants that facts, results, testimonials, ratings, claims, disclaimers, and legal statements Client supplies are accurate and substantiated. Consultant may refuse to publish material Consultant reasonably believes is false, misleading, unsupported, or non-compliant.

3.8 Solicitation. Client is responsible for compliance with rules on direct solicitation in each jurisdiction where Client advertises or practices. Consultant will not knowingly initiate prohibited solicitation on Client’s behalf.

3.9 Supervision of Nonlawyers. Client acknowledges its duty to supervise nonlawyer assistants and marketing vendors who prepare or place marketing materials. Consultant will cooperate with reasonable supervision and documentation requests.

3.10 Jurisdiction-Specific Rules. Client is responsible for advertising rules, data/privacy compliance, TCPA/CAN-SPAM compliance, and marketing regulations where Client is licensed and where ads run. Consultant is not Client’s ethics counsel or privacy counsel.

3.11 Pre-Existing Systems. Consultant is not responsible for defects, data loss, misconfiguration, compliance gaps, reporting failures, or account limitations in systems, accounts, or data that existed before Consultant’s access, except to the extent an SOW expressly scopes remediation.

ARTICLE 4. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

4.1 Access and Data. Client will provide timely admin access to agreed tools, including advertising accounts, CRM, analytics, website, call tracking, practice-management systems, exports, reporting downloads, billing screens, and access screens reasonably required to perform and invoice the work.

4.2 Assets and Approvals. Client will provide brand assets, disclaimers, logos, legal review, and attorney advertising approval. A standing approval process or named designee may apply across SOWs until revoked.

4.3 Primary Contact. Client will designate one primary contact authorized to approve scope, creative, access, and invoices.

4.4 Payment. Client will pay all amounts due under this Agreement, each SOW, and each Schedule. Late or unpaid amounts may pause digital ads stewardship and all consulting work under open SOWs as stated in Article 6. Partial payment is better than none and does not waive the remaining balance unless Consultant agrees in writing.

ARTICLE 5. FEES, EXPENSES, TAXES, AND PAYMENT

5.1 Fees. Fees are set in each SOW and may use Consultant’s payment plan codes (P0-P5), deposit ladder, deferred payment terms, retainer terms, and bonus schedules.

5.2 Standard Terms. Unless an SOW states otherwise, invoices are Net 15. Deposits are non-refundable after kickoff. Consultant may pause work after ten (10) days’ notice of overdue amounts.

5.3 Late Charges. Past-due amounts accrue a late charge of 1.5% per month (or the maximum allowed by law, if lower).

5.4 Taxes. Client pays applicable sales/use tax on taxable services or tangible goods under applicable law. Consultant is responsible for Consultant’s own income taxes and employment taxes. Platform media spend billed directly to Client by third parties is outside Consultant’s invoices unless Consultant resells a taxable good. If a taxing authority later assesses tax on a pass-through item, Client will reimburse the tax and documented interest/penalty caused by Client’s failure to pay when due.

5.5 Pass-Through Hard Costs. Pre-approved receipted costs (print, materials, shipping, local pickup, etc.) are invoiced at cost plus the handling fee in Section 5.6, unless the SOW states “at cost only.”

5.6 Physical Coordination / Pickup Fee. When Consultant personally designs, sources, picks up, stages, or manages physical supplies, Consultant may charge: (a) [15]% of receipted hard costs or a flat $[75] per trip, whichever is greater; and/or (b) a pre-approved fixed change-order if coordination exceeds [1] hour of Consultant time.

5.7 Deferred Payment. Extended or large deferrals (including P2 or any deferral beyond the standard P1 remainder timing) require written Schedule A terms. Because this Agreement uses the co-client structure, the Individual Co-Client is jointly and severally responsible for deferred balances unless Consultant expressly waives that responsibility in the SOW.

5.8 Performance Bonuses. If the Parties attach Schedule B (or another written bonus schedule) under Section 3.5, bonuses invoice when the metric is documented. Bonuses are not drawn from trust accounts and are not calculated as a share of legal fees, case value, settlements, or signed cases unless Client’s ethics counsel approves in writing. Schedule B is optional and is not required for a valid SOW.

ARTICLE 6. DEFAULT, COLLECTIONS, AND REMEDIES

6.1 Default. Client is in default if any invoice, deferred balance, premium, pass-through amount, late charge, or other amount remains unpaid after the due date and any cure period stated in the SOW or Schedule.

6.2 Acceleration. Upon default, termination for Client breach, Firm insolvency, Firm dissolution, or Firm bankruptcy filing, all unpaid amounts under open SOWs become immediately due to the maximum extent permitted by law, including deferred balances, earned fees, premiums then due under Schedule A, pass-throughs, late charges, and transition fees.

6.3 Suspension. Consultant may suspend work, withhold unpublished deliverables, pause transition support, and stop non-emergency account management while amounts remain overdue. Client remains responsible for platform accounts, ad spend, and business operations during suspension.

6.4 Collection Costs and Attorney Fees. Client will pay all reasonable costs of collection, including collection-agency fees, court costs, service fees, reasonable attorney fees, and reasonable fees incurred to enforce or interpret this Agreement, to the extent permitted by law and awarded or recoverable under applicable procedure.

6.5 Collection Agency. Consultant may refer overdue amounts to a collection agency after written notice if payment is not made within [10] days after the notice.

6.6 Court Remedies. Consultant may file suit in small claims, county court, district court, or another court of competent jurisdiction depending on the amount owed and applicable law. In Colorado, small claims is generally limited to $7,500 exclusive of interest and costs; if the balance exceeds that amount, Consultant may choose a higher court or waive the excess only if Consultant decides to do so.

6.7 Demand Letter. Before referral to collections or filing suit, Consultant may send a demand letter separating principal, late charges, deferred premiums, pass-throughs, collection costs, and any requested attorney fees.

6.8 No Waiver. Accepting partial payment does not waive Consultant’s right to collect the balance unless Consultant agrees in writing.

INTERNAL RESEARCH NOTES — STRIP FROM CLIENT PDF / GOOGLE DOC

Co-client structure: Firm + individual sign as Co-Clients with joint/several liability; cleaner than separate guarantee when Andrew is intended to be directly liable.

Signed-case-rate bonuses: High ethics risk for outside marketing vendors. Default: do not bonus on signed cases, signed clients, or legal fees. Use fixed-dollar process/marketing KPIs unless ethics counsel approves.

Collections: Colorado small claims limit generally $7,500 exclusive of interest/costs; larger balances may require county/district court or waiver of excess. Include collection costs/attorney fees in signed contract if you want to seek them.

Late fees: 1.5%/month is common; ensure not punitive under applicable law.

Ad vendor fees: Reasonable marketing/vendor costs generally allowed; not pay-for-recommendation or fee splitting.
