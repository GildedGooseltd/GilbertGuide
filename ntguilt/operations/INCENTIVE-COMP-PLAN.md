## ⚠️ Legal constraint (read once)

Compensation for nonlawyer staff may use **salary / hourly**, **flat $** tied to activity metrics, or **flat $** per qualified consult held — **not** a percentage of legal fees, retainers, settlements, or collections.

## Role model — Commission-only sales (can equal full-time pay)

**Goal:** A nonlawyer outbound / intake-sales role can earn **full-time living pay from commissions alone** (optional small draw), without a full salary — while staying inside Colorado fee-sharing rules.

### Short answer: % of fee / trust — no · lifecycle tiers — yes, as flat $

| Idea | Allowed? | Why |
|------|----------|-----|
| **% of retainer, case fee, or money in trust** | **No** | Colorado HB26-1421 + RPC **5.4** — pay may not be contingent on a **percentage of legal fees/revenues** or determined by reference to recoveries / case economics |
| **Higher flat $ as the client moves down the funnel** | **Yes (counsel-approve)** | Same *shape* as sales commission, but each stage pays a **predetermined flat** written before the period — not a formula on that client’s fee |
| **Use avg case value only to set the flat schedule** | **Yes (owner math)** | Firm may reverse-engineer flats from “we want ~$[___]/mo at expected volume” — worker still sees fixed $ per stage, not “5% of trust deposit” |
| **Trigger pay when funds hit trust** | **Risky if $ scales with deposit** | Prefer: flat $ when **engagement signed + first qualifying payment posted** (minimum defined by firm). Counsel must sign off if using “trust funded” as a gate |

Full memo: [LEGAL-MARKETING-COMP-COMPLIANCE.md](LEGAL-MARKETING-COMP-COMPLIANCE.md)

### Lifecycle bounty ladder (compliant pattern)

Pay **more dollars at later stages** — not higher *percentages of the fee*.

| Stage | Event (define precisely) | Relative weight (example) | Flat bounty (draft) | Proof |
|-------|--------------------------|---------------------------|---------------------|-------|
| **L1** | Qualified lead attributed to seller | Low | **$[___]** | HubSpot create + attribution |
| **L2** | Consult **booked** | Medium | **$[___]** | Calendar + HubSpot |
| **L3** | Consult **held** | Medium–high | **$[___]** | Attorney confirms occurred |
| **L4** | **Matter opened / engagement signed** | High | **$[___]** | MyCase + signed agreement |
| **L5** | **First qualifying funds posted** (ops account or trust per counsel) | Highest flat | **$[___]** fixed — **not** % of deposit | Accounting / trust ledger |

**Stacking:** One person can earn L1→L5 on the same matter **only if** each stage is distinctly proven and attribution is locked. Cap: **$[___]/matter** and/or **$[___]/mo**.

**Do not write on the schedule:** “2% of retainer” · “10% of money in trust” · “bonus = fee × tier %.”

### How to calculate rates so commissions ≈ full-time salary

**Owner worksheet** (internal — not shown as % to the worker):

1. Pick **target monthly cash** for a productive seller = full-time equivalent take-home goal → **$[___]/mo** (example: replace a $[___]/yr salary ÷ 12).  
2. Estimate **expected monthly volume** at each stage for that role (from HubSpot/MyCase history).  
3. Assign **weight shares** across L1–L5 that sum to 100% of target pay (example below).  
4. **Flat at stage** ≈ `(target monthly cash × weight) ÷ expected units at that stage`. Round to clean dollars.  
5. Publish **only the flat table** to the worker. Recalibrate quarterly if volume or mix changes — still flats, never live % of fees.

**Example weights (edit):**

| Stage | Weight of target pay | If target = $4,000/mo and expected units | Implied flat |
|-------|----------------------|------------------------------------------|--------------|
| L1 Lead | 10% ($400) | 40 leads | **$10** |
| L2 Booked | 15% ($600) | 12 booked | **$50** |
| L3 Held | 20% ($800) | 10 held | **$80** |
| L4 Signed | 30% ($1,200) | 4 signed | **$300** |
| L5 First funds posted | 25% ($1,000) | 3 funded | **$333** → round **$325** |

At expected volume, pay ≈ **$4,000/mo** with **$0 salary**. Above-expected volume → above FTE; below → below (optional draw softens floor).

**Draw (optional):** Small weekly advance against commissions; reconcile monthly. Not a salary — an advance.

**Classification:** Prefer **W-2 commission + optional draw** unless counsel approves true independent contractor. No “1099 employee.”

### Funnel KPIs this role owns (re-add to locked list if paying)

Outbound dials · connect rate · consults booked · consults held · attributed new matters · first payment posted (ops metric for L5 gate).

**Not paid on:** answered-phone KPI 21 (intake/phone) · revenue % · settlement % · trust balance %.

## Design principles (keep short)

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Leading indicators first | Leads, answer rate, speed-to-task, consults — before revenue |
| Pay on defined units | Flat $ or pool tiers — never % of legal fees |
| One scoreboard | HubSpot + MyCase + Ads exports = source of truth |
| Owner metrics stay owner | Revenue / collections inform strategy, not nonlawyer fee share |
