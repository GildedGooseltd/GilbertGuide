## Layers

### Layer 1 — What should marketing fix first?
1. Get more qualified cases → paid / campaign demand  
2. Make the website earn its keep → SEO, speed, landers  
3. See leads, spend, and ROI clearly → dashboards & audits  
4. Stop leaking money → ads & subscriptions  

### Layer 2 → Layer 3 (examples)

**Cases / demand**
- Channel: search & LSA · display/NTGUILT · seasonal · warm past-client  
- After click/call: VoIP · after-hours chat · HubSpot pipeline · lead-source tracking  
- Warm channel: referral program · testimonials · mailer · directories/social  

**Website**
- Weakest: SEO/content · speed/nav · landing pages  
- SEO workstream: content overhaul · blog · local/directories  

**Clarity**
- Measurable: live KPIs · fund-next audit · cash leak  
- KPI focus: build dashboard · monthly reporting  

**Costs**
- Waste: SaaS/cards · ad targeting · full clean-up  
- Ad waste: enhance existing · seasonal rebuild · ads retainer
