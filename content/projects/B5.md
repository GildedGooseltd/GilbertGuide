## Description

Snailmail postcard campaign using the insurance sleeves as the mailer. Puts Pav Law in front of previous clients and increases referral and repeat potential. Summer is peak driving and ticket season — a physical reminder in the car supports a long-tail campaign. Sleeves plus postage keep cost under ~$3 per piece while the reminder stays in the glovebox.

**Note: Tracking the ROI for this campaign is going to be very difficult.**

## Goal

Generate 1–2 retained matters per ~200-household wave at ≤$3/piece all-in cost.
