## Summary

Reconcile the firm’s full operating spend across QuickBooks, bank and credit-card statements, ad platforms, software, phone systems, payroll, contractors, sponsorships, and vendor agreements. Turn the subscription waste already observed into a verified monthly and annual savings plan.

## Waste already identified

| Finding | Amount already visible | Audit treatment |
| ------- | ---------------------- | --------------- |
| Non-client Google Search terms | **$1,063 in 30 days** | Confirm query-level waste, add negatives, and prevent recurrence |
| Safe Military Display negative-keyword subset | **About $694 in 30 days** | Included inside the $1,063 finding; do not double-count |
| Unused or overlapping subscriptions | **Multiple charges observed; dollar total not yet verified** | Inventory every recurring charge and calculate monthly + annual waste from statements |
| Phone lines and overlapping systems | **Not yet quantified** | Match each line/license to an owner and use; cancel or consolidate idle seats |
| LSA disputes, credits, and billing leakage | **Not yet quantified** | Reconcile charged leads, disputes, credits, and booked/signed outcomes |

**Known waste floor:** $1,063 in one 30-day ad-spend review, equal to **$12,756 annualized if it repeats**. This is not the total audit opportunity: it excludes subscription cancellations, duplicate software, unused phone lines, uncredited LSA charges, staffing leakage, and vendor overpayment.

## Value Added

- Produce a transaction-level spend ledger by vendor, owner, department, payment method, and contract term
- Inventory every recurring subscription, software seat, phone line, domain, hosting plan, directory, and ad tool
- Label each charge **keep · renegotiate · consolidate · cancel · dispute · needs owner**
- Cancel or replace unused SaaS and overlapping tools; document confirmation numbers and effective dates
- Reconcile ad-platform spend and credits against bank and credit-card charges
- Reconcile LSA charged leads, disputes, credits, booked leads, and signed cases
- Review payroll, contractor, sponsorship, event, and commission costs against actual use and measurable return
- Build a 30-day savings action list plus monthly and annual run-rate savings forecast
- Add renewal dates and owners so waste does not return

## Payment plan

- **Fixed project fee:** $1,800
- **Deposit:** 50% due at signing; remainder under the selected Guide schedule
- **Savings share:** Gilded Goose receives **20% of verified net savings or money recovered** from a recommendation or implementation completed through this project

| Financial-management change | Savings-share period |
| --------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Subscription, SaaS seat, phone line, domain, hosting, directory, or other recurring-service cancellation / downgrade / consolidation | **12 months from the effective change date** |
| Vendor, telecom, insurance, processing, or other continuing contract-rate revision | **6 months from the effective change date** |
| Advertising, LSA, staffing-schedule, or other variable operating-process revision | **3 months after implementation**, adjusted for comparable volume |
| Refund, credit, disputed charge, duplicate payment, or other recovery | **One 20% fee when the cash or credit is actually received** |

**Calculation:** verified net savings = signed pre-change baseline minus actual post-change cost, less direct implementation costs, termination fees, replacement costs, taxes, and third-party fees.

Recurring savings shares are invoiced quarterly in arrears from statements or vendor invoices. No implemented change or no realized savings means no savings-share fee. Reversed changes stop accruing on the reversal date. Overlapping savings are counted once.

The savings share excludes reductions caused only by lower business volume, client-only decisions, market-price changes, taxes, debt principal, legal fees, settlements, case value, trust funds, or firm revenue. The obligation survives project completion or termination only through the applicable 12-, 6-, or 3-month period.

**Contract requirement:** attach the Savings-Share Rider from `SCHEDULES-PAYMENT.md` to the B9 SOW and have counsel review the first signed use.

## Success measures

- 100% of recurring charges assigned to an owner and business purpose
- All unnecessary subscriptions and idle seats canceled or scheduled for termination
- Every identified credit or dispute submitted and tracked
- Monthly and annual savings shown from verified statements, without double-counting
- Renewal calendar and approval rule in place for future software and vendor purchases
