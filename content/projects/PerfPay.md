## Romina — collectible pace support tier

Use collectible pace vs the approved monthly or mid-year target as a scorecard indicator for a fixed-dollar pay-for-performance tier. Pair it with actions Romina can verify and control.

| Component | Draft rule |
|-----------|------------|
| Pace indicator | At or above the approved collectible target for the measurement period |
| Intake gate | New matters, payment-plan status, and first-payment follow-up recorded on time |
| Follow-up gate | Assigned overdue-payment or missing-document tasks completed by deadline |
| Verification | MyCase + accounting export; QuickBooks actual cash replaces the current 80% estimate when available |
| Payout shape | Predetermined flat-dollar tier only — never a percentage of fees, trust, or collections |

The current +$67,043 vs mid-year target is evidence that the indicator is useful, not a permanent baseline. Unknown business debt and the use of estimated collections mean it cannot trigger a payout by itself.
