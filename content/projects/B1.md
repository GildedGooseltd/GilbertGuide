## Description

A web lead only becomes revenue if intake moves fast. Research on [speed to lead](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/inbound-sales) shows response within five minutes dramatically improves qualification rates — yet Pav Law's HubSpot workflows, Romina booking link, and referral import path are not fully connected. This sprint proves the full path: form submit → task → bookable consult.

Speed to lead matters — firms that respond within minutes qualify far more inquiries than those that wait hours. HubSpot workflow automation routes form fills to tasks and booking links so intake is consistent, not ad hoc. See [HubSpot Academy — automation](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/automation), [HubSpot — speed to lead statistics](https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/inbound-sales), and [Law firm CRM setup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4yM_GJPHB8E).
