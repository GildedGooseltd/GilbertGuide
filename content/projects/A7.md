## Description

NTGUILT event creative is already paid for — but sitting unused in paid channels means that investment generates no calls. Ad strength and fresh responsive search ads directly affect click-through rate; fatigued creative quietly raises cost per click.

Ad creative fatigues over time — Google’s ad strength guidance shows how fresh responsive search ads and Display assets improve click-through at the same budget. Repurposing existing creative is a standard way to refresh without a full redesign. See Google — ad strength in Search, Google — responsive search ads, and Responsive search ads.
