## Description

People search specific questions before they call a lawyer — "what happens after a DUI stop," military traffic rules, and similar long-tail queries. Existing blog posts can rank for those searches if titles, meta descriptions, and internal links point readers to practice pages. This is the content SEO layer on top of technical site work.

Content SEO targets questions people search before they call a lawyer — blog posts with clear titles, meta descriptions, and internal links pass authority to practice pages. Clio’s SEO guide and Google’s article structured data docs explain the pattern.
